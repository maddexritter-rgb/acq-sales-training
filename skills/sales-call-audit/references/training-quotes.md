# Training quotations

Use the active brand profile and quote bank. The reusable skill does not prescribe a trainer identity.

Choose a short excerpt whose original context supports the specific recommendation. A quotation reinforces the reasoning; it does not replace the call evidence or prove what caused the outcome. Default to one quote for the priority issue, with a second only when materially useful. Do not sprinkle quotes through every section.

Render: “<exact excerpt>” — <verified speaker or document title>. Follow with one sentence applying it to the rep's observed behavior. No training-video links, URLs, timestamp citations, raw source IDs, or internal file paths in customer feedback. Call timestamps and call links are distinct and may still be included. Supply provenance separately if explicitly requested.

Before using a quotation, confirm exact wording against the retained source and review surrounding context. Preserve meaningful wording; capitalization and punctuation may be normalized for readability. Never merge separate passages into one quotation, polish a paraphrase into a quote, or assign a document passage to a named person without verified authorship. Auto-caption matches are transcript-verified, not audio-verified. Omit uncertain or garbled wording; report source limits if relevant or asked.

Quotes from a handbook/workbook are attributed to that document unless authorship is established. Quotes from other speakers' call videos are not the brand trainer's words. Only ingested and verified sources are eligible; a handbook's links to additional materials do not establish that those materials were reviewed.

Extend the bank as useful passages are verified. Record exact excerpt, speaker/document attribution, source title and locator, source ID, surrounding-context summary, verification level, applicable coaching situations, and misuse risks. Keep full source transcripts and private call content outside the distributable package. Respect quotation limits, aggregating excerpts from the same source within a response.
