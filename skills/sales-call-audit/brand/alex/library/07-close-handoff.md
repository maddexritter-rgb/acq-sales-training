# Commitments, follow-up, and onboarding

Original coaching adaptations. Suggested dialogue is newly written, not a source quotation. Source ranges identify the reviewed teaching sections supporting this module. Each card has a distinct trigger; apply only when the call evidence fits.



## CLS01 — Ask for the next appropriate commitment

**Notice:** The rep gives useful information but never invites a decision.

**Change:** Make one clear request matched to the call's purpose.

**Suggested wording:** “Would it make sense to book the specialist conversation?”

**Next branch:** If yes, arrange it; if concern, address that concern; if no, respect it.

**Practice check:** One unambiguous stage-appropriate ask appears.

**Boundary:** A setter does not need to ask for payment.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS02 — Leave room after the ask

**Notice:** The rep answers their own question or adds a second pitch before a response.

**Change:** End the request and wait for the buyer's answer.

**Suggested wording:** “Would the next step be useful? [wait]”

**Next branch:** If they answer, follow the answer; if the line is unclear, check the connection.

**Practice check:** In practice, the rep stops speaking after the ask.

**Boundary:** Do not claim an exact original pause length without reliable audio timing.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS03 — Stop reselling after yes

**Notice:** The buyer agrees and the rep introduces new objections with extra pitch.

**Change:** Move to the agreed action and confirm practical details.

**Suggested wording:** “Great. The next action is [agreed step]. Here is what is needed to complete it.”

**Next branch:** If a new concern appears, answer it; otherwise complete the action.

**Practice check:** No new benefit monologue follows an unambiguous yes.

**Boundary:** A yes to learning more is not a yes to purchase.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS04 — Distinguish intent from completion

**Notice:** The buyer says an assistant will pay later and the rep calls it closed.

**Change:** Record the promise separately from actual payment or booking confirmation.

**Suggested wording:** “The link is sent; payment is still pending. Once confirmed, the team can complete the booking.”

**Next branch:** If paid is verified, mark it accordingly; if promised only, keep pending.

**Practice check:** State exactly what was observed, promised, or independently confirmed.

**Boundary:** A buyer saying good sale is not proof of collected cash.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS05 — Use secure purchase steps

**Notice:** The buyer is ready to pay and a payment workflow is needed.

**Change:** Direct them to the business's approved secure payment channel.

**Suggested wording:** “The approved secure link handles payment. No card details need to be shared in this chat.”

**Next branch:** If the link fails, use approved support; if uncertain, verify the destination.

**Practice check:** No card number, security code, or identity document is requested by the coach.

**Boundary:** Do not copy source advice about collecting a card before informed agreement.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS06 — Book a useful follow-up

**Notice:** The call ends with send something or talk later.

**Change:** Agree the purpose, participants, day, time, timezone, and preparation if the buyer wants a meeting.

**Suggested wording:** “The next call would settle [open issue]. What time works with the necessary participant?”

**Next branch:** If agreed, confirm invite receipt when observable; if declined, do not force it.

**Practice check:** A follow-up has a specific unresolved question and accepted time.

**Boundary:** Do not mutate a calendar or CRM without separate authorization.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS07 — Hand off the actual context

**Notice:** The buyer must explain the same problem again after buying.

**Change:** Pass the confirmed goal, relevant facts, promises, open risks, and next owner through an authorized process.

**Suggested wording:** “The delivery team should receive the confirmed goal and the commitments made on this call.”

**Next branch:** If handoff is documented, preserve it; if missing, draft the summary for review.

**Practice check:** The next owner can state the buyer's goal without redoing discovery.

**Boundary:** Do not share private call data outside the authorized workflow.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS08 — Make onboarding concrete

**Notice:** A buyer says yes but does not know what happens next.

**Change:** Explain the first action, owner, timing, and how to get help.

**Suggested wording:** “Next comes [verified step] from [owner] by [confirmed timing]. If it does not arrive, use [approved contact].”

**Next branch:** If timing is uncertain, verify it; if delivery has started, confirm status.

**Practice check:** The buyer knows the first action and who owns it.

**Boundary:** Do not guess preparation time or arrival dates.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS09 — Ask for referrals at the right moment

**Notice:** A satisfied customer expresses appreciation or a referral request is appropriate.

**Change:** Ask for a permission-based introduction connected to a relevant need.

**Suggested wording:** “Is there someone with a similar issue who would welcome an introduction?”

**Next branch:** If yes, let the customer make or authorize it; if no, move on.

**Practice check:** A referral request is optional and does not delay delivery.

**Boundary:** Do not request private contacts without consent or imply referrals are owed.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)



## CLS10 — Reinforce through delivery, not hype

**Notice:** Post-sale uncertainty appears or a team treats payment as the end.

**Change:** Confirm expectations and make the first promised experience happen.

**Suggested wording:** “The most useful reassurance now is completing the agreed first step and confirming who will help.”

**Next branch:** If expectations conflict, resolve them; if aligned, follow through.

**Practice check:** Check the promised first action rather than adding more motivational pitch.

**Boundary:** Do not promise that an onboarding gesture guarantees retention.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 1208–1329; V2 lines 5561–5582 (10717.5–10753.4s); V2 lines 8002–8233 (15630.7–16064.1s)
