# Practice and team coaching

Original coaching adaptations. Suggested dialogue is newly written, not a source quotation. Source ranges identify the reviewed teaching sections supporting this module. Each card has a distinct trigger; apply only when the call evidence fits.



## MGR01 — Choose one practice priority

**Notice:** A review lists many faults without a behavior to rehearse.

**Change:** Select the most consequential supported issue and model the alternative.

**Suggested wording:** “The practice target is accepting a corrected premise before the next question.”

**Next branch:** If the learner can perform it, vary the buyer answer; if not, repeat this behavior.

**Practice check:** Name one visible pass criterion before the drill.

**Boundary:** A large library should produce focused feedback, not a hundred-point report.

## MGR02 — Let the learner actually respond

**Notice:** The coach writes both sides and calls the exercise completed.

**Change:** Present one buyer turn and wait for the learner.

**Suggested wording:** “Buyer: We already receive the reports. That is not what is missing.”

**Next branch:** If the learner acknowledges the correction, advance; if not, correct that one move.

**Practice check:** Practice is complete only after a real learner attempt.

**Boundary:** A written demonstration is not evidence of trained skill.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR03 — Correct promptly and specifically

**Notice:** Feedback arrives long after the rep has repeated the error.

**Change:** Point to the exact phrase and model one change immediately.

**Suggested wording:** “The next question still assumes missing reports. Try a question about execution instead.”

**Next branch:** If corrected, repeat; if not, reduce the task to one sentence.

**Practice check:** The learner knows which words or choice to change.

**Boundary:** Do not attack personality or motivation.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR04 — Vary the buyer after repetition

**Notice:** The rep can recite a response but cannot adapt.

**Change:** Rehearse the same skill with a different underlying buyer need.

**Suggested wording:** “Buyer: The reports are fine; the managers disagree about who acts on them.”

**Next branch:** If the rep changes the next question, pass the variant; otherwise return to diagnosis.

**Practice check:** Pass across different answers, not just identical repetition.

**Boundary:** Drill success does not establish better sales conversion.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR05 — Use wins and losses together

**Notice:** A team copies everything from a closed deal or only studies failures.

**Change:** Compare the same behavior across comparable successful and unsuccessful calls.

**Suggested wording:** “What did the rep do consistently, and what differed in the buyer's situation?”

**Next branch:** If the behavior appears in both, avoid causal claims; if pattern is promising, test prospectively.

**Practice check:** Retain one counterexample for every proposed winning pattern.

**Boundary:** One sale cannot validate a catchphrase.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR06 — Run a useful game-tape review

**Notice:** A group piles criticism onto a rep without a specific next action.

**Change:** Let the rep identify a change, preserve an evidenced strength, then rehearse one moment.

**Suggested wording:** “At this timestamp, what would the rep do differently next time?”

**Next branch:** If the diagnosis fits the evidence, practice; if not, replay the context.

**Practice check:** The review ends with a demonstrated behavior, not just discussion.

**Boundary:** Do not publish private recordings or employee rankings by default.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR07 — Train listening as well as speaking

**Notice:** Practice covers pitch recitation but not interpreting answers.

**Change:** Have the learner summarize the buyer's last answer before choosing a response.

**Suggested wording:** “State the buyer's problem in one sentence, then ask the next useful question.”

**Next branch:** If accurate, continue; if not, replay and correct.

**Practice check:** The next question follows the actual answer, including corrections.

**Boundary:** Knowing the product script is not the same as knowing the prospect.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR08 — Assess readiness before live opportunities

**Notice:** A new rep is sent to calls based only on days employed.

**Change:** Check accurate offer facts, recap ability, common branches, and secure next-step handling.

**Suggested wording:** “The rep should demonstrate these skills on varied practice cases before owning the full call.”

**Next branch:** If a critical gap remains, coach it; if ready, use supported live review.

**Practice check:** Use observed readiness criteria rather than a fixed-day guarantee.

**Boundary:** Do not recommend firing from a single call or source anecdote.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR09 — Separate coaching skill from closing skill

**Notice:** A top closer is assumed able to train everyone else.

**Change:** Observe whether the person can diagnose, model, correct, and repeat a behavior with another rep.

**Suggested wording:** “Show how a learner improves after one specific correction.”

**Next branch:** If they can coach, develop that role; if not, provide a coaching process or other support.

**Practice check:** Evaluate learner improvement, not only the trainer's own sales.

**Boundary:** Do not infer management ability from charisma or revenue alone.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 185–420; V1 lines 623–742 (1300.4–1549.2s); V2 lines 7501–7824 (14625.2–15297.7s); V2 lines 8501–8725 (16578.3–16993.0s)



## MGR10 — Bring delivery feedback into sales training

**Notice:** Reps repeat claims that delivery cannot fulfill or lose conviction from unresolved complaints.

**Change:** Review actual customer outcomes and delivery scope with the responsible team.

**Suggested wording:** “Which promises are consistently delivered, and which need correcting in the sales conversation?”

**Next branch:** If a promise is unsupported, revise it; if delivery fails, fix the operational issue.

**Practice check:** One feedback loop connects sales promises to delivery evidence.

**Boundary:** Testimonials alone do not establish typical results or remove customer risk.
