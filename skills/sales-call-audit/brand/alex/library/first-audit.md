# Example: the first response after a call upload

Fictional demonstration. Neither the buyer nor the transcript below is a real prospect. This illustrates the response shape, not a measured model benchmark.

**Call supplied:** A staffing-services discovery call. The buyer says new hires wait days for instructions. The rep recommends recruiting more people. The buyer replies that enough people have already been hired; supervisors do not give them clear assignments. The rep asks how costly the hiring shortage has been. The buyer ends the call without a next meeting.

---

The rep kept selling against a hiring shortage after the buyer corrected it. The next question needed to be about assigning work.

**Replay — 04:12.** The buyer said, “We have enough people. They don't know what to do when they start.” At 04:25, the rep asked about the cost of not having enough people. That question returned to a problem the buyer had just ruled out. The call ended without a next step; the transcript does not establish whether this caused the ending.

**Change this.** Accept the correction before asking anything else. Alex Hormozi puts the principle simply: “you want to understand not win.” The rep needs to understand who assigns work before deciding whether this service fits.

**Suggested rep response:**

> “Got it—the hiring is done. The gap is getting new people productive. Who is supposed to give them their first assignment?” **[wait]**

If the buyer names a supervisor:

> “What does that supervisor give a new hire on day one?” **[wait]**

If nobody owns it:

> “So the first issue is ownership. Before discussing recruiting, the team needs to check whether this service actually helps build that onboarding process.”

If the service only recruits people, the rep should say so. Better wording cannot make the wrong offer fit.

**Keep:** The rep asked about the business before presenting the service. Keep that opening; change how the rep uses the answer.

**Recommended practice:** Give the rep three different corrections to an initial diagnosis. The rep passes when the next question uses the corrected facts without returning to the rejected premise. Start with: “We have enough people. They don't know what to do when they start.” The manager can run the drill, or the rep can request interactive practice here.
