# Wording, delivery, and script repair

Original coaching adaptations. Suggested dialogue is newly written, not a source quotation. Source ranges identify the reviewed teaching sections supporting this module. Each card has a distinct trigger; apply only when the call evidence fits.



## DEL01 — Separate words from delivery

**Notice:** The coach attributes a weak outcome to tone from transcript alone.

**Change:** Assess wording from text and reserve acoustic judgments for audio.

**Suggested wording:** “Suggested delivery: ask the short question, then leave space for the answer.”

**Next branch:** If audio exists, inspect the relevant moment; if not, label delivery unobserved.

**Practice check:** Every tone claim points to audible evidence or is labeled a future instruction.

**Boundary:** No invented confidence, sarcasm, pitch, or emotion from text.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL02 — Use a speakable sentence

**Notice:** The rep's response contains stacked clauses and abstract nouns.

**Change:** Rewrite one thought in plain language and end with one question.

**Suggested wording:** “What happens when the manager is not there?”

**Next branch:** If the answer is concrete, follow it; if vague, ask for an example.

**Practice check:** Say the line aloud without losing its purpose.

**Boundary:** Shorter is useful only if material facts remain intact.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL03 — Ask one question at a time

**Notice:** The buyer answers only part of a multi-part question.

**Change:** Split the question at the decision point.

**Suggested wording:** “Who owns the training today? [wait]”

**Next branch:** If ownership is clear, ask about process next; if not, explore ownership.

**Practice check:** The rep waits for one answer before asking the next question.

**Boundary:** Do not turn the call into a rigid interrogation.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL04 — Replace abstract impact questions

**Notice:** The rep repeatedly asks how it impacts the buyer after an impact was stated.

**Change:** Use the known impact and ask the next unresolved operational question.

**Suggested wording:** “Expansion depends on the top rep traveling. What would a new hire need to do independently?”

**Next branch:** If the dependency is confirmed, discuss the capability gap; if corrected, revise.

**Practice check:** Each follow-up advances beyond the previous answer.

**Boundary:** Do not ask the buyer to repeat pain merely to satisfy a script slot.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL05 — Practice pace for comprehension

**Notice:** The buyer cannot follow a dense explanation and audio supports excessive speed or poor clarity.

**Change:** Shorten the passage and rehearse with clear boundaries between ideas.

**Suggested wording:** “The offer has two relevant parts. First, [part one]. [pause] Second, [part two].”

**Next branch:** If comprehension improves, preserve the change; if not, revise the wording.

**Practice check:** A listener can accurately repeat the explanation after hearing it.

**Boundary:** No universal words-per-minute pass/fail threshold.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL06 — Use pitch and emphasis deliberately

**Notice:** Audio reveals unclear question boundaries or emphasis that changes meaning.

**Change:** Model the intended question and rehearse the specific words in context.

**Suggested wording:** “What would need to change for the team to handle that without the owner?”

**Next branch:** If it sounds accusatory, simplify and soften; if clear, move to the next skill.

**Practice check:** Compare the original and practiced phrase for intelligibility and intended meaning.

**Boundary:** Pitch measurements alone do not establish persuasion or emotion.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL07 — Memorize to free attention

**Notice:** The rep reads rigidly and misses a buyer correction.

**Change:** Practice the base sequence until familiar, then introduce varied buyer answers.

**Suggested wording:** “That changes the picture. Let me check the revised understanding.”

**Next branch:** If the rep adapts accurately, continue; if not, rehearse the listening branch.

**Practice check:** Correctly respond to three different answers without repeating a contradicted premise.

**Boundary:** Do not replace an approved live script without review.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL08 — Rewrite the decision, not just the adjective

**Notice:** A proposed script sounds smoother but makes the same wrong assumption.

**Change:** Change the question or branch that caused the problem before polishing tone.

**Suggested wording:** “The buyer already has reports. Ask what happens after the reports arrive.”

**Next branch:** If the diagnosis changes, rewrite the next branch; if wording alone is unclear, tighten it.

**Practice check:** Explain which decision the new wording changes.

**Boundary:** Do not claim imitation of a trainer's voice is evidence of quality.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL09 — Keep quote and suggested dialogue distinct

**Notice:** Generated replacement language is presented as if the trainer said it.

**Change:** Label the new line as original suggested wording; use only the verified quote bank for attribution.

**Suggested wording:** “Suggested rep response: [original wording grounded in this call].”

**Next branch:** If an exact verified quote fits, use it briefly; otherwise omit it.

**Practice check:** No generated script is attributed to the trainer as a quote.

**Boundary:** Do not reconstruct unpublished source passages for quotation requests.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)



## DEL10 — Recover from losing the script

**Notice:** The rep loses their place and restarts discovery or rambles.

**Change:** Return to the last confirmed milestone and identify the next missing fact.

**Suggested wording:** “The goal and the obstacle are clear. The remaining question is who would implement the change.”

**Next branch:** If confirmed, ask that question; if not, correct the milestone.

**Practice check:** Recover without restarting answered questions.

**Boundary:** A framework guides the conversation; it does not override the buyer's answer.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 573–762; V1 lines 2124–2337 (4350.6–4788.8s); V2 lines 5588–5792 (10764.8–11169.4s)
