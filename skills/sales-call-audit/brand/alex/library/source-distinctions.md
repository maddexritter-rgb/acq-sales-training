# Apply the right version of the teaching

These resolutions are coaching implementation choices after full retained-text review, not new quotations from the sources.

| Tension in the materials | How this coach handles it |
|---|---|
| Handbook emphasizes exact script fluency; courses also teach question-based milestones and reasoning | Preserve the approved base script while adapting the next question to the actual answer. Requested rewrites are drafts for review. |
| Handbook objection loop and video AAA use different middle steps | Keep them distinct. Acknowledge the actual concern, add truthful relevant information, check whether it resolved the concern, then make a suitable invitation. Association never permits invented social proof. |
| Pause after asking versus stacking persuasive perspectives | Give the buyer room to answer. A second perspective must respond to unresolved evidence, not fill every silence. Connection failure is a separate possibility. |
| Money as a value problem versus genuine affordability | Diagnose available resources, timing, value, and risk separately. No pressure toward debt or sacrificing essentials. |
| Time as priorities versus real capacity and prerequisites | Ask what cannot happen now and what changes later. Account for workload, staffing, care obligations, and dependencies. |
| Stakeholder objection versus joint decision authority | Include legitimate decision-makers. Do not use secrecy, forgiveness language, or assumed stakeholder intent. |
| Short pitch and three pillars versus adequate scope disclosure | Keep the explanation concise; do not force exactly three needs, omit effort, or hide material terms. |
| Diagnostic sale personalized to a goal versus guaranteed outcomes | Match actual capabilities to the confirmed goal. A customer selecting a desired state does not make an outcome certain or authorize a charge. |
| Prepayment framing and recurring transitions | Show full cost, payment schedule, and any renewal explicitly using current approved terms. No hidden continuity or pre-consent card collection. |
| Conviction and testimonials versus product problems | Use verified results and delivery feedback. If support is deficient, fix the promise or product; do not coach the rep to sound certain about an unsupported claim. |
| Exact pace, pause, timeline, conversion, compensation, and revenue figures | Treat these as historical examples or claims, not validated universal benchmarks. The diagnostic-sale passage also contains arithmetic inconsistencies; recompute any figures used. |
| Lower close rate in a larger funnel | Compare total qualified throughput, cash, and downstream quality using consistent cohorts. A higher filtered close rate can coexist with fewer total sales. |
| Cut weak performers rapidly versus coaching evidence | Assess repeated comparable behavior, role requirements, support, and readiness. This skill does not make firing decisions from one recording. |
| Recording/compliance statements and historical software recommendations | Follow the user's applicable recording process; the library does not claim universal legal rules or that historical products remain best. |
| Cold-call demonstration versus trainer teaching | The third supplied video is call-practice evidence. Its speaker's commentary is not an Alex quote or an automatically validated coaching rule. Deduplicate its opening teaser from the later full call. |

The public library intentionally excludes copied internal scripts, private operating thresholds, historical offer promises, coercive examples, and unsupported universal claims. This leaves the underlying decision skills usable across businesses without disclosing the paid source text.
