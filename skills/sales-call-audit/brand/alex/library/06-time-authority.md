# Timing, authority, and complex decisions

Original coaching adaptations. Suggested dialogue is newly written, not a source quotation. Source ranges identify the reviewed teaching sections supporting this module. Each card has a distinct trigger; apply only when the call evidence fits.



## TIM01 — Separate calendar from capacity

**Notice:** The buyer says not now.

**Change:** Ask whether the obstacle is a date conflict, time to implement, or a decision still being evaluated.

**Suggested wording:** “Is the issue attending on that date, doing the work afterward, or deciding whether to proceed?”

**Next branch:** If date, check approved availability; if capacity, map effort; if decision, clarify criteria.

**Practice check:** Choose the next question from the actual timing subtype.

**Boundary:** Do not dismiss all time constraints as priorities.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM02 — Plan around real workload

**Notice:** The buyer cannot assign staff or hours to implementation.

**Change:** Clarify the actual work and available capacity before recommending a start.

**Suggested wording:** “Who would own the first step, and when could they realistically do it?”

**Next branch:** If no owner or capacity, defer or change scope; if available, document the plan.

**Practice check:** Identify owner and capacity without inventing a time-saving feature.

**Boundary:** Buying does not automatically create implementation time.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM03 — Distinguish seasonal delay

**Notice:** The buyer cites a busy season or major event.

**Change:** Ask what changes afterward and whether any preparation is useful now.

**Suggested wording:** “What becomes possible after that period that is not possible today?”

**Next branch:** If a real change exists, plan around it; if uncertain, explore rather than accuse.

**Practice check:** Record the actual change that makes a later date useful.

**Boundary:** Do not assume waiting is avoidance.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM04 — Verify prerequisites

**Notice:** The buyer must hire, secure approval, or complete another project first.

**Change:** Determine whether the prerequisite truly affects delivery or purchase.

**Suggested wording:** “Which part of this depends on that being finished?”

**Next branch:** If necessary, sequence it first; if not necessary, explain the verified independence.

**Practice check:** Draw a simple dependency before recommending a start.

**Boundary:** Do not invent urgency to bypass a real dependency.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM05 — Respect time to decide

**Notice:** The buyer needs to compare options or review terms.

**Change:** Identify the needed information and agree a reasonable decision process.

**Suggested wording:** “What information should be available before the next conversation is worthwhile?”

**Next branch:** If requested, provide it; if no follow-up wanted, leave the decision with the buyer.

**Practice check:** A follow-up has a reason beyond asking again.

**Boundary:** Time for research is different from time to implement.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM06 — Map the other decision-maker

**Notice:** The buyer says a partner, spouse, or manager must be involved.

**Change:** Clarify their role and what they need to evaluate.

**Suggested wording:** “What part of the decision will they own, and what questions will they want answered?”

**Next branch:** If joint decision, include them; if delegated authority, confirm the buyer's scope.

**Practice check:** Name each participant's decision role without assuming opposition.

**Boundary:** Never suggest concealing a purchase or bypassing shared authority.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM07 — Equip a champion without guessing

**Notice:** The buyer will relay the offer internally.

**Change:** Create a factual summary of problem, scope, terms, evidence, and open questions.

**Suggested wording:** “What would make this summary useful for that conversation?”

**Next branch:** If they need a specialist, offer one if available; if summary suffices, keep it short.

**Practice check:** The summary contains no invented stakeholder objections.

**Boundary:** Do not make the buyer promise someone else's approval.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM08 — Book the stakeholder conversation

**Notice:** Progress requires another participant and the buyer wants to continue.

**Change:** Agree purpose, attendees, preparation, and a feasible time.

**Suggested wording:** “Would a short meeting with the implementation owner help settle the support question?”

**Next branch:** If yes, propose actual available times; if no, agree an appropriate alternative.

**Practice check:** The next meeting has a decision to make and the right people present.

**Boundary:** Do not count an unaccepted invite as a confirmed meeting.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM09 — Handle procurement as a process

**Notice:** An enterprise deal needs security, legal, budget, or procurement review.

**Change:** Map milestones, owners, evidence, and dependencies instead of forcing a one-call close.

**Suggested wording:** “What must be approved, by whom, before a purchase can happen?”

**Next branch:** If review is defined, support it; if unclear, find the process owner.

**Practice check:** Document the next verifiable milestone and owner.

**Boundary:** A required review is not automatically a stall.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)



## TIM10 — Keep availability truthful

**Notice:** The rep claims scarcity or deadlines to overcome timing concerns.

**Change:** Use only verified capacity, dates, and expiration terms.

**Suggested wording:** “The confirmed opening is [date]. If it does not fit, the team can check later availability.”

**Next branch:** If no verified deadline, omit urgency; if real, explain its basis.

**Practice check:** Every urgency statement has a current factual basis.

**Boundary:** Do not use historical course dates or fake busy calendars.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1208–1232; V2 lines 1016–1884 (2102.0–3799.1s); V2 lines 3134–3229 (6235.7–6417.7s)
