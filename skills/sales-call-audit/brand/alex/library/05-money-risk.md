# Money, value, and purchase risk

Original coaching adaptations. Suggested dialogue is newly written, not a source quotation. Source ranges identify the reviewed teaching sections supporting this module. Each card has a distinct trigger; apply only when the call evidence fits.



## MON01 — Separate affordability from value

**Notice:** The buyer says they cannot afford it.

**Change:** Ask whether the issue is available funds, timing, or whether the result merits the cost.

**Suggested wording:** “Is the difficulty the available budget, when funds are available, or whether this is worth the price?”

**Next branch:** If budget is absent, respect it; if value is unclear, revisit the problem-to-offer link.

**Practice check:** Choose one money branch using the buyer's answer.

**Boundary:** Do not assume everyone can find the money.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON02 — Handle genuine budget limits

**Notice:** The buyer confirms that the purchase is outside available resources.

**Change:** Offer only an appropriate approved lower-scope option or a future conversation if wanted.

**Suggested wording:** “Given that limit, this option may not fit. Would a smaller approved scope be useful to compare?”

**Next branch:** If no suitable option, end cleanly; if one exists, explain changed scope.

**Practice check:** No recommendation to sacrifice essentials or take debt to force a sale.

**Boundary:** Motivational source analogies are not financial guidance.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON03 — Handle cash timing

**Notice:** The buyer wants the offer and identifies a concrete funding date.

**Change:** Clarify timing and use only documented payment or follow-up options.

**Suggested wording:** “When will the decision and funds be available, and would a check-in then be useful?”

**Next branch:** If an approved arrangement fits, explain total terms; if not, follow up at the agreed time.

**Practice check:** Record date, purpose, and conditions rather than an assumed commitment.

**Boundary:** Do not invent a delayed charge or refundable deposit.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON04 — Rebuild the value explanation

**Notice:** The buyer cannot connect the offer to a worthwhile business change.

**Change:** Return to the confirmed problem and explain the actual mechanism.

**Suggested wording:** “The service only makes sense if [verified capability] helps with [stated bottleneck]. Is that connection clear?”

**Next branch:** If unclear, explain or admit mismatch; if clear, explore the remaining concern.

**Practice check:** Use the buyer's goal instead of a generic value multiple.

**Boundary:** No guaranteed ROI or invented income increase.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON05 — Address a previous bad purchase

**Notice:** The buyer has paid for something similar that disappointed.

**Change:** Ask what was promised, what happened, and what evidence would distinguish this offer.

**Suggested wording:** “What was missing last time that would need to be different here?”

**Next branch:** If the same limitation exists, disclose it; if different, show verified scope or proof.

**Practice check:** Name the actual difference and its evidence.

**Boundary:** Do not blame the buyer for not believing enough.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON06 — Make guarantees precise

**Notice:** The rep uses risk-free language or a vague guarantee.

**Change:** State actual eligibility, conditions, remedy, exclusions, and who confirms them.

**Suggested wording:** “The guarantee covers [approved remedy] under [conditions]. It does not remove the time required.”

**Next branch:** If terms are unavailable, verify before claiming coverage; if excluded, say so.

**Practice check:** The replacement script contains no broader promise than the approved terms.

**Boundary:** A refund policy does not eliminate all risk.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON07 — Compare the cost of waiting honestly

**Notice:** The rep claims waiting will definitely cost a specific sum.

**Change:** Use known current costs and disclose uncertainty about the future.

**Suggested wording:** “If this continues at the current measured rate, the estimated cost is [calculation]. What might change that?”

**Next branch:** If inputs are uncertain, show a range or omit the number; if reliable, compare options.

**Practice check:** Show the inputs and arithmetic behind any estimate.

**Boundary:** Do not guarantee either deterioration or improvement.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON08 — Present approved payment choices clearly

**Notice:** The rep improvises discounts or obscures the total commitment.

**Change:** Show approved total price, installment amounts, fees, and timing side by side.

**Suggested wording:** “The approved options are [A total and schedule] or [B total and schedule]. Which, if either, fits?”

**Next branch:** If neither fits, stop; if one fits, confirm the full terms.

**Practice check:** The buyer can distinguish payment amount from total price.

**Boundary:** Do not relabel financing costs to hide them.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON09 — Downscope without pretending equivalence

**Notice:** A smaller offer is available for a buyer with a narrower need or budget.

**Change:** Explain exactly what is removed and what outcome remains supported.

**Suggested wording:** “This smaller option includes [scope] and excludes [scope]. Would that still solve the priority issue?”

**Next branch:** If essential support is removed, do not recommend it; if sufficient, compare honestly.

**Practice check:** State one meaningful tradeoff before offering the lower price.

**Boundary:** A discount is not an automatic substitute for a fit diagnosis.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)



## MON10 — Turn vague fear into a checkable risk

**Notice:** The buyer worries the purchase will not work but cannot name why.

**Change:** Ask which failure they are concerned about and what would reduce uncertainty.

**Suggested wording:** “What is the outcome the buyer most wants to avoid here?”

**Next branch:** If evidence can address it, provide it; if uncertainty remains, acknowledge it.

**Practice check:** Identify a specific risk and an appropriate evidence request.

**Boundary:** Do not describe all hesitation as irrational fear.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 986–1035; H1 lines 1045–1207; V1 lines 2744–3078 (5551.0–6199.1s); V2 lines 459–1015 (973.8–2099.9s)
