# Context and call readiness

Original coaching adaptations. Suggested dialogue is newly written, not a source quotation. Source ranges identify the reviewed teaching sections supporting this module. Each card has a distinct trigger; apply only when the call evidence fits.



## CTX01 — Identify the call's job

**Notice:** A setter, follow-up, or onboarding call is judged as a failed close.

**Change:** Name the intended commitment from the opening and prior context before evaluating missing stages.

**Suggested wording:** “The next step here is a conversation with the specialist. Would that be useful?”

**Next branch:** If the job is booking, assess booking; if closing, assess a buying decision.

**Practice check:** State the job and one matching success criterion before scoring.

**Boundary:** Do not invent a call objective from a CRM label alone.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX02 — Use the existing notes

**Notice:** The buyer repeats facts already supplied in an application or earlier call.

**Change:** Confirm the relevant prior fact, then ask what changed.

**Suggested wording:** “The earlier notes say expansion stalled at hiring. Is that still the issue?”

**Next branch:** If confirmed, investigate the current gap; if corrected, replace the old note.

**Practice check:** Use one verified prior fact without making the buyer retell the whole story.

**Boundary:** Missing notes are unknown context, not proof the rep failed.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX03 — Repair caller confusion

**Notice:** The buyer asks who is calling or does not recognize the resource.

**Change:** Restate identity and the actual reason in one sentence before discovery.

**Suggested wording:** “This is the team following up on the business guide requested. Does that ring a bell?”

**Next branch:** If yes, ask permission to continue; if no, explain briefly and offer to end.

**Practice check:** The buyer can identify the caller and reason before the next discovery question.

**Boundary:** Never imply a relationship or request that is not supported.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX04 — Check available attention

**Notice:** The buyer is driving, distracted, or repeatedly says time is short.

**Change:** Offer a short purposeful conversation or a later time; do not press for detailed figures.

**Suggested wording:** “It sounds like now is awkward. Would a better time make this easier?”

**Next branch:** If available, narrow the agenda; if unavailable, agree a later contact only if wanted.

**Practice check:** Ask the availability question and follow the answer.

**Boundary:** Do not infer consent to a long call from answering the phone.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX05 — Fix connection before diagnosing

**Notice:** Repeated requests to repeat or statements about cutting out appear.

**Change:** Pause the sales sequence, check whether speech is coming through, and offer a reconnection.

**Suggested wording:** “The line keeps cutting out. Should we reconnect, or find another time?”

**Next branch:** If repaired, recap the last confirmed fact; if not, stop the substantive pitch.

**Practice check:** After two explicit hearing problems, address the connection before another sales question.

**Boundary:** Silence after price is not a price objection without a buyer response.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX06 — Use relevant credibility

**Notice:** The rep lists company status while the buyer's problem remains unclear.

**Change:** Give only credibility that explains why this conversation is relevant, then return to the buyer.

**Suggested wording:** “The team works on this type of bottleneck. Where is it showing up in this business?”

**Next branch:** If the claim is verified, keep it brief; otherwise omit it.

**Practice check:** Each credibility statement connects to a stated buyer problem.

**Boundary:** Company size does not prove this offer fits.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX07 — Set a useful agenda

**Notice:** The conversation drifts because neither side knows the intended decision.

**Change:** Explain the sequence and possible next step, including that no fit is possible.

**Suggested wording:** “First, clarify the bottleneck. Then check whether the service fits. If it does, discuss the next step.”

**Next branch:** If the buyer wants a different agenda, agree it; if time is short, reduce scope.

**Practice check:** Deliver the agenda in two sentences and get agreement.

**Boundary:** Do not manufacture a promise to buy before discovery.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX08 — Separate cold from pre-sold

**Notice:** The buyer already consumed training or had detailed discovery.

**Change:** Ask what is already understood and what still needs resolving.

**Suggested wording:** “What is already clear about the service, and what still needs an answer?”

**Next branch:** If well informed, focus on remaining concerns; if unfamiliar, explain essentials.

**Practice check:** Avoid repeating a pitch the buyer can already summarize accurately.

**Boundary:** Do not infer comprehension merely from a sent video link.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX09 — Prepare for the person

**Notice:** The rep asks broad questions despite relevant known business context.

**Change:** Review the stated goal, last conversation, and promised follow-up before the call.

**Suggested wording:** “Last time the concern was training new hires. What happened with that since then?”

**Next branch:** If still open, continue there; if resolved, find the current priority.

**Practice check:** Write a three-line pre-call brief: goal, open issue, promised next step.

**Boundary:** Research should not become intrusive speculation about private finances.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)



## CTX10 — Know the offer before promising

**Notice:** The rep is uncertain about support, terms, eligibility, or dates.

**Change:** Use an approved offer facts sheet; mark unknowns for confirmation.

**Suggested wording:** “That detail needs checking. The confirmed part is [known fact]. Can the team verify the remaining point before a decision?”

**Next branch:** If verified, answer directly; if unknown, assign an owner and return time.

**Practice check:** Separate confirmed facts from assumptions in every proposed script.

**Boundary:** Do not fill missing offer details with the training company's historical terms.

**Teaching sources:** H1 lines 443–453; H1 lines 763–811; V1 lines 1381–1679 (2900.0–3504.5s)
